VoiceISO removes background noise from your microphone, on your own computer.
Nothing is sent over the internet.


FIRST LAUNCH

Mac: drag VoiceISO to Applications and open it. macOS will refuse the first time
because the app isn't registered with Apple. Open System Settings > Privacy &
Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway". Then click Allow when it asks
for the microphone.

Windows: run the installer. If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click
"More info", then "Run anyway". No administrator rights are needed.

Loading takes a few seconds. The status changes from "starting" to "running".


TO HEAR IT YOURSELF

Plug in headphones, click Rescan, and speak. Speakers are muted at first because
they would feed back into the microphone.


TO USE IT IN GOOGLE MEET OR ZOOM

This needs a free virtual-microphone driver, installed once. The app shows a
link to it if it's missing.

  Mac:     install BlackHole 2ch, click Rescan in VoiceISO, then choose
           "BlackHole 2ch" as the microphone in the call app.
           In a browser, use Chrome or Brave. Safari does not work with it.

  Windows: install VB-CABLE, click Rescan in VoiceISO, then choose
           "CABLE Output" as the microphone in the call app.

Keep VoiceISO open during the call. Turn off the call app's own noise
cancellation so you hear VoiceISO's result.


GOOD TO KNOW

Closing the window keeps VoiceISO running. To stop it, click its icon in the
menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows) and choose Quit.

Mac version: Apple Silicon only (M1 or newer).
Windows version: 64-bit Intel or AMD.

Log file, if something goes wrong:
  Mac:     ~/Library/Logs/VoiceISO/voiceiso.log
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\VoiceISO\voiceiso.log
